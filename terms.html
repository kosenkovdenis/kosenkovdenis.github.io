Personal utility

Outlook calendar sync is a personal utility maintained for its owner's use. It is not offered as a public subscription service.

The application connects calendars that the owner is authorized to access and copies Exchange events into the Google Calendar account selected during sign-in. The owner chooses the synchronization settings and controls whether the application is running.

Availability

Synchronization depends on the computer being awake, the application running, network availability, and valid access to both calendar services. Google Calendar copies may therefore lag behind Exchange. Exchange remains the source calendar for synchronized meetings.

Stopping use

The owner can stop using the application at any time by stopping the program and removing its autostart task. Google access can be revoked through Google Account settings. Previously copied events remain in Google Calendar until removed by the owner or the application's synchronization rules.

Data processing is described in the Privacy Policy.
